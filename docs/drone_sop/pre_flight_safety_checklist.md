# DGCA 15-Point Pre-Flight Safety Inspection SOP

1. **Airframe**: Inspect carbon fiber arms, motor mounts, and landing gear for micro-fractures.
2. **Propellers**: Check 30-inch folding blades for nicks; torque hub screws to 1.8 N-m.
3. **Motors**: Hand-rotate each brushless motor to detect bearing friction or debris.
4. **LiPo Battery**: Verify cell voltage delta < 0.03V; physical pack temperature < 35°C.
5. **Avionics**: Confirm DroneCAN dual GPS compass alignment and RTK fixed status (> 16 satellites).
6. **Airspace Clearance**: Verify Digital Sky green zone authorization token loaded on ground station.
7. **Telemetry Link**: Verify 2.4 GHz control link RSSI > -75 dBm and 4G cellular backup active.
8. **Failsafe Settings**: Verify Return-To-Home (RTH) altitude set to 25m AGL (clearing tree lines).
9. **Spray Payload**: Inspect 10L tank quick-release clamp, filter basket, and manifold pressure relief valve.
10. **Plumbing**: Pressure test diaphragm pump at 0.35 MPa; verify zero hose leakage.
11. **Nozzles**: Flush TeeJet flat-fan nozzles with clean water; check spray pattern uniformity.
12. **Flowmeter**: Calibrate hall-effect sensor pulse count against 1.0L test container.
13. **Weather Check**: Confirm ambient wind speed < 12 km/h and Delta T between 2 and 8.
14. **Geofencing**: Verify 5-meter buffer margin inside field boundary survey polygon.
15. **Emergency Protocols**: Verify pilot physical kill-switch disarm function and fire extinguisher on site.
