# AgriVision — Farmer Personas & Social Impact

## 1. Primary User Personas

### Persona A: Rammohan Kumar (Smallholder Farmer)
- **Location**: Patna Rural, Bihar
- **Holding**: 12.5 Acres (Wheat & Mustard rotation)
- **Key Pain Point**: Cannot identify early Yellow Rust stripe symptoms; relies on local pesticide retailers who sell over-priced, toxic broad-spectrum chemicals.
- **AgriVision Solution**: Uses the Bhojpuri/Hindi voice interface to book a Rs 450 drone scan. Receives a targeted 0.92-acre spray prescription, saving Rs 14,200 per season.

### Persona B: Amit Singh (Certified Drone Pilot)
- **Location**: Danapur, Bihar
- **Equipment**: DJI Agras T40 with RTK base station
- **Key Pain Point**: Struggles to find steady farmer demand during non-peak seasons.
- **AgriVision Solution**: Receives automated mission dispatches via AgriVision Operator Dashboard with pre-computed flight waypoints and instant UPI payouts.

---

## 2. Measurable Social & Environmental Impact
1. **Chemical Reduction**: 38% average reduction in active pesticide volume via localized spot-spraying.
2. **Water Conservation**: Drip scheduling alerts save approximately 62,000 liters of water per acre per season.
3. **Farmer Net Income**: 18-22% increase in net profit margins due to chemical cost savings and early crop salvage.
