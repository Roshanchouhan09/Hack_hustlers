# AgriVision — Sustainability & Economic ROI Model

## 1. Economic ROI for Smallholder Farmers (10-Acre Model)

| Cost Component | Conventional Farming | AgriVision Precision System | Net Savings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blanket Pesticide Cost | Rs 28,000 | Rs 11,500 (Spot spray) | **Rs 16,500** |
| Manual Labor for Spraying | Rs 8,000 | Rs 4,500 (Drone scan & spray) | **Rs 3,500** |
| Crop Loss due to Delayed Detection | Rs 35,000 (15% loss) | Rs 7,000 (3% loss) | **Rs 28,000** |
| **Total Expenditure & Loss** | **Rs 71,000** | **Rs 23,000** | **Rs 48,000 / Season** |

## 2. Environmental & ESG Alignment
- **UN Sustainable Development Goals**:
  - **SDG 2**: Zero Hunger (improving food security and crop yield).
  - **SDG 6**: Clean Water and Sanitation (stopping pesticide groundwater runoff).
  - **SDG 12**: Responsible Consumption and Production.
  - **SDG 13**: Climate Action (electric battery drones replacing fossil-fuel tractor sprayers).
- **Carbon Offset**: Electric drone spraying eliminates diesel tractor emissions, saving an estimated 42 kg CO2e per hectare annually.
