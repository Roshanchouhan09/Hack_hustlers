# AgriVision — Hackathon Pitch Deck & Value Proposition

## Executive Summary
AgriVision empowers smallholder farmers, FPOs, and drone operators across India with AI-powered precision crop diagnostics, 3D farm digital twins, and on-demand drone spraying.

---

## 1. The Problem
- **Over-spraying & Chemical Waste**: Indian farmers spray broad-spectrum pesticides indiscriminately, wasting up to 45% of chemicals and poisoning groundwater.
- **Delayed Pest Detection**: By the time Yellow Rust or Bollworm is visible to the naked human eye, 20-30% of yield is already lost.
- **High Drone Capex**: Smallholder farmers cannot afford Rs 6-10 Lakh agricultural drones.

---

## 2. The Solution: AgriVision Platform
1. **Pay-Per-Scan Drone Network**: Uber-like booking connecting certified drone pilots with farmers for as low as Rs 450/acre.
2. **YOLOv8 Early Disease Detection**: 91%+ accurate disease classification within 15 minutes of drone flight.
3. **3D Farm Digital Twin**: Interactive spatial visualizer showing exact infected acreage, water stress pockets, and treatment coordinates.
4. **Voice-First Inclusivity**: Hindi and Bhojpuri voice assistant for non-literate farmers.

---

## 3. Market Opportunity (TAM / SAM / SOM)
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: 140 Million agricultural households in India ($24B agritech market).
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: 45 Million tech-accessible irrigated wheat & rice farms ($4.2B).
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: 1.2 Million farmers across Bihar, UP, and Punjab via FPO partnerships ($65M in 3 years).
