# ICAR 3-Season Cropping Calendar for Indo-Gangetic Plains

### 1. Kharif Season (Monsoon: June - October)
- **Primary Crops**: Paddy (Oryza sativa), Maize, Soybean, Cotton, Pearl Millet.
- **Water Management**: High rainfall dependence; drainage channels crucial during monsoon depressions.

### 2. Rabi Season (Winter: November - April)
- **Primary Crops**: Wheat (Triticum aestivum), Chickpea, Mustard, Barley, Potato.
- **Irrigation Regime**: 4-6 timed irrigations synchronized with CRI, tillering, and flowering stages.

### 3. Zaid Season (Summer: March - June)
- **Primary Crops**: Moong Bean (Green Gram), Watermelon, Cucumber, Fodder Sorghum.
- **Critical Factor**: Short duration, high heat tolerance, and micro-sprinkler or drip irrigation.
