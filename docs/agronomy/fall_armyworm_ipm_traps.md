# Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) Pheromone Grid & Biocontrol

### Surveillance & Economic Threshold Level (ETL):
- Deploy 4 Gossyplure pheromone delta traps per acre at a height of 1.0 meter above the crop canopy.
- Trap catch threshold: 3 moths/trap/night indicates active oviposition.

### Bio-Agent Inundative Release:
1. **Egg Parasitoid**: Release *Trichogramma chilonis* cards at 50,000 parasitized eggs/acre at 7 and 14 days after germination.
2. **Bio-Pesticide**: Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) var. *kurstaki* at 2.0 g/L or Beauveria bassiana (1x10^8 CFU/g) at 5 g/L during evening hours.
