# ICAR 6 Critical Growth Stages for Wheat Irrigation

Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) requires 4 to 6 irrigations during the Rabi season depending on soil type:

1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)**: 20-25 days after sowing (DAS). **Most critical stage**. Yield drops by up to 35% if missed.
2. **Tillering Stage**: 40-45 DAS. Promotes effective tillers per plant.
3. **Late Jointing Stage**: 60-65 DAS. Determines stem elongation and earhead size.
4. **Flowering / Anthesis Stage**: 80-85 DAS. Critical for pollination; avoid moisture stress or water stagnation.
5. **Milking Stage**: 100-105 DAS. Starch accumulation in grains begins.
6. **Dough Stage**: 115-120 DAS. Final grain hardening; light irrigation only to avoid lodging under strong March winds.
