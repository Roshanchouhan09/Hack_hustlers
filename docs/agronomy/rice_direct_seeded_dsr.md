# Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) Agronomy & Weed Management

Direct Seeded Rice (Tar-Vattar method) saves up to 25% irrigation water and reduces methane emissions by 40% compared to flooded puddling.

### Critical Management Practices:
1. **Field Preparation**: Laser land leveling is mandatory to ensure uniform seed placement at 2-3 cm depth.
2. **Seed Rate**: 20-25 kg/ha primed seed treated with Trichoderma viride (4 g/kg).
3. **Pre-Emergence Herbicide**: Spray Pendimethalin 30% EC at 2.5 L/ha within 24-48 hours of sowing on moist soil.
4. **Post-Emergence Herbicide**: Apply Bispyribac-sodium 10% SC at 200 ml/ha at 20-25 days after sowing to suppress grassy and broadleaf weeds.
5. **First Irrigation**: Delay first irrigation until 15-21 days after sowing to encourage deep root elongation.
