# AgriVision — 3-Minute Hackathon Winning Demo Script

## Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
> *"Judges, 140 million farmers in India lose up to 30% of their crop value to pests and fungi every year simply because they detect it too late, and then over-compensate by dumping toxic, expensive chemicals across their entire field.*
> *Today, we present **AgriVision** — an AI-powered precision agriculture platform that combines autonomous agricultural drones, YOLOv8 computer vision, and interactive 3D digital twins to spot diseases at the millimeter level and guide precision spot spraying."*

---

## The Demo (0:30 - 2:00)

### 1. 3D Farm Digital Twin (`/farms/1/health-map`)
> *"Instead of flat, confusing 2D maps, AgriVision gives farmers and agronomists a full 3D spatial digital twin. Here you can see Green Valley Farm in Patna. Notice our autonomous DJI Agras T40 drone actively scanning the crop canopy using high-resolution LiDAR and multispectral lasers.*
> *Right here in the north-east corner, our 3D warning beacon pulses in red — our YOLOv8 model detected Yellow Rust with 91% confidence, but notice that it only affects 0.92 acres out of 12.5! Rather than wasting Rs 28,000 spraying the whole farm, we spot-spray just that sector for under Rs 2,500."*

### 2. 3D Plant Leaf Inspector & Prescription
> *"Clicking on the disease hotspot zooms in directly to our 3D Wheat Leaf pathology inspector, showing the exact parallel uredinial pustules rupturing the leaf epidermis and generating an automated agronomist prescription for Propiconazole 25% EC."*

### 3. Voice & Inclusive Access
> *"For smallholder farmers who speak regional dialects, our voice assistant understands Hindi and Bhojpuri with native agricultural vernacular."*
